Powering AI Agents with StreamHouse to manage Inventory & demand in real time

Demo guide

This page is a live view of a store during a busy holiday season. Sales, promotions, and stock levels update as they happen, across all product lines, all regions, and all channels (stores or online).

Forecast and stock

The chart compares units sold with the forecast. When a location is about to run out, the agent recommends a move or an order.

Humans and AI

Humans and an AI agent can track demand and manage inventory round the clock. The agent suggests moving stock from a location that has plenty to one that is running low, or ordering more. A person can approve each move, or let the agent act on its own.

Try it

Pick a product, then start a promo or the Black Friday demo. Watch the chart, the low-stock list, and the recommendations change.
